## mrt_sales__monthly_growth

Computes month-over-month revenue growth rate by store.

**Status**: explored but not used in the final dashboard. Growth rate
becomes highly volatile and misleading when the prior month's revenue
is very low (e.g. due to the data completeness gap from May 2018
onward), producing extreme percentages that don't reflect real business
trends. Kept in the project as a documented example of an analytical
path that was tested and discarded, rather than removed silently.
